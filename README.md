One word for the whole internet, funniest and most fitting wins.

## The word

**unhinged**

The internet is not a library, a town square, or a shopping mall. It is
everyone shouting at once, at 3 a.m., about everything, forever — memes,
arguments, conspiracy threads, cat videos, and strangers ranking each
other's sandwiches. "Unhinged" is the one word that makes you laugh and
then nod, because it is true of the whole thing at once: the chaos is the
point, and the chaos never logs off.

## Entry

`main.py` (standard library only) writes the word to `/output/entry.txt`:

```bash
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
# unhinged
```
